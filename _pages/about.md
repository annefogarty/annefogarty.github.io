I am an economics PhD student at the University of California, Berkeley. My research interests span health, industrial organization, and public finance. My work is supported by the National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship.


Previously, I was a predoctoral research fellow at the Harvard Kennedy School in the [Health Inequality Lab](https://www.healthinequalitylab.org/) where I contributed to projects examining the determinants and consequences of health disparities in the United States. 


You can reach me at annefogarty@berkeley.edu.
